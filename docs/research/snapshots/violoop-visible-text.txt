What is Violoop, and how is it different from ChatGPT or other AI assistants? + Violoop is not a chatbot. It's a plug-in AI device that operates on your actual computer — it can open applications, manage files, execute code, browse the web, run scheduled tasks, and work in the background while you stay focused on what matters. Most AI assistants respond to prompts and stop there. Violoop executes and keeps going. What operating systems does Violoop support? + Violoop supports Windows, macOS, and Linux. Do I need a technical background to use Violoop? + No. Violoop is designed to be useful whether you're a developer running scripts or a creator organizing project files. You interact with it in plain language. The depth you get out of it scales with the complexity of what you ask. Does Violoop send my screen or files to the cloud? + Violoop reads and interprets your screen on the device itself — the raw video feed never leaves it. For tasks that need a large model, Violoop sends a filtered text summary of the relevant context to a cloud AI service. It does not stream your screen and does not upload your files. Actions on your computer are executed through a standard keyboard-and-mouse connection, and nothing executes until you press the key. Where is my data stored? + Your files stay on your machine. Task history and memory are stored locally. We are building toward fully configurable data residency options for users who need stricter control over what leaves their environment.
